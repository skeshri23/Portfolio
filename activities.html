Activities and Interests

BuckeyeThon Dance Marathon
August 2024 - February 2025

I am proud to have participated in BuckeyeThon, The Ohio State University’s largest student-run philanthropy. After months of fundraising to support children with pediatric cancer and blood disorders, our team from the Society of Women Engineers (SWE) placed among the Top 8 Miracle Cup finalists and secured a spot in the Top 5 for fundraising, raising $17,932! This incredible effort helped BuckeyeThon raise a total of $853,710 for children being treated in the Hematology, Oncology, and Blood & Marrow Transplant Unit at Nationwide Children's Hospital in Columbus, Ohio.

The Medical and Economic Aspects of Robot-Assisted Surgery
May 2024

I analyzed the medical benefits and economic challenges of robotic surgery, examining its impact on patient outcomes and healthcare costs. My research explored disparities in access between developed and developing countries, focusing on the U.S. and India. I evaluated ethical implications using Act Utilitarianism and incorporated recent market statistics and advancements, demonstrating in-depth research and critical analysis of the field.
